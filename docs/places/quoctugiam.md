# Quốc Tử Giám (id `quoctugiam`)

Model: `src/landmarks/places/quoctugiam.js`. Before this, the site was only the generic mapped buildings: a 9.5 m
tiled block for Di Luân Đường and plain blocks for the side halls. No wall, gate or court. The new model covers those
footprints, so `populate` drops the generic versions, and no guard in an older builder was needed.

## Confirmed and used
- Di Luân Đường:
  - The OSM plan (20.4 m wide, 26.9 m deep, about 549 m²) is close to the 574 m² in the record.
  - It faces south-east, toward đường 23 Tháng 8, which is the site's address. The two Học halls flank the court in front of it.
  - Two storeys, a two-tier roof in yellow glaze, 3 × 5 steps on each face with dragon strings on the middle flights, and the cổ diêm frieze.
- The Học halls are long, single-storey, tiled and lime-washed, with timber doors. The Giám sinh halls are light yellow.
- The stele shelter stands in front of the hall.

## Estimated
- Di Luân Đường:
  - the split between the front house and the main house
  - storey and eave heights
  - the upper storey's size (10.4 m square) and the colours of the mosaic panels
  - the plain-tile faces, drawn as a darker yellow
- Tam quan: form, size, colour and roof. Model: three doors, a raised middle roof, ochre lime, yellow tiles. Its position on the axis at the front wall is also estimated.
- Wall: height (2.4 m) and colour. It is drawn 2.5 m inside the OSM precinct line. The two side gates are left out because their positions are unknown.
- Side halls: terracotta roofs, verandah columns on the Học halls' court side, and which outbuilding is which. The rear north-east pair is taken as the Giám sinh halls.
- Paving: the brick apron and the axial walk. The rest of the court is left as lawn with the generated trees.
