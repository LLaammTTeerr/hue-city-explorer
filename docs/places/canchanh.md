# Điện Cần Chánh (id `canchanh`)

Model: `src/landmarks/places/canchanh.js`. It draws the restored platform, its three front flights, the stone column
bases, the lim timber frame now standing on them, and the paved court (sân bái mạng) in front. No older builder drew
this place (only its label existed), so no guard was added.

## Sources
- OSM way 285990343 (historic=ruins, "Điện Cần Chánh"): the platform outline, 44.5 × 35.3 m. OSM steps
  619851677, 619851679 and 619851678 are the three front flights, at u ≈ -15.9, -0.4 and +14.0 m. The court lies
  between Tả Vu (way 172657574) and Hữu Vu (way 172657572).
- vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Điện_Cần_Chánh_(Hoàng_thành_Huế): built 1804. Platform nearly 1 m high, faced with gạch vồ
  and Thanh stone. Front hall 7 gian 2 chái đơn, main hall 5 gian 2 chái kép, 80 lim columns. Razed in February 1947.
  Restoration ground broken 23 Nov 2024.
- khamphahue.com.vn (9 Mar 2026), "Foundation reinforcement prior to Điện Cần Chánh restoration": curbs dismantled,
  stone bases marked, a reinforced-concrete footing system. Works run Apr 2025 to Mar 2029, target Nov 2028.
- baovanhoa.vn "Di tích điện Cần Chánh dần hiện rõ giữa Tử Cấm thành" (17 Aug 2026): frame nearly 60 % up. 90 lim
  columns, 80 in the main building and 10 under the mái lưa (9 bays). Roofing from about October 2026.
- vov.vn/van-hoa/dien-can-chanh-dan-phuc-hoi-sau-gan-80-nam-bi-pha-huy-post1325693.vov (20 Aug 2026): foundation
  done, frame 60 %, main building over 1,400 m².
- tuoitre.vn/can-canh-dien-can-chanh-hoi-sinh-giua-tu-cam-thanh-10026083113284048.htm (31 Aug 2026): frame taking
  shape. Finish to be red lacquer and gilt. A construction fence carries historical photo panels.

## Confirmed
- The plan type matches Thái Hòa (trùng thiềm điệp ốc), with the same bay counts. Column total is 90.
- The outline and the positions of the three front flights come from OSM.
- Reconstruction is under way in 2026: the foundation is finished, the timber frame stands, and roof work began
  about October 2026.

## Estimated
- **Frame state in Nov 2026:** all 90 columns up, with plates, tie beams, principal rafters, purlins and ridge
  beams. No battens, tiles or lacquer yet; the timber is drawn as raw lim brown.
- **Column grid:** Thái Hòa's bay proportions scaled to a 41 m span. Depth rows were fitted to the 35.3 m platform,
  and the mái lưa row sits 1.6 m inside the back edge.
- **Heights:** floor 1.15 m above the ground and 0.93 m above the court. Column and roof heights copied from
  Thái Hòa.
- **Steps:** 5 steps each, about 2 m run. The OSM ways are 2.1 m long.
- **Court:** stone paving raised 0.22 m, with a central ngự đạo and kerbs on the open sides. Its extent runs from
  the platform to Đại Cung Môn's foundation at v ≈ 46 m.
- **Not drawn:** the construction fence, scaffolding, any temporary roof and the site workshop. Their positions and
  forms are unknown. Back and side steps are also left out (not mapped).
