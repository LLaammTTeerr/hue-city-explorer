# Cầu Dã Viên (place id `bachho`)

Replica: `src/landmarks/places/bachho.js`. Guards in `src/geo/city.js` skip the
generic Dã Viên pavilions, the generic piers of the north-channel road spans and
the generic Bạch Hổ / Dã Viên rail bridges.

## What stands here today
The brief said the bridge was "rebuilt 2014-2017, replacing the old Bạch Hổ rail
bridge". That is not what the sources say. Two structures stand side by side:
- **Cầu Dã Viên road bridge**, a concrete girder bridge opened 2 Sept 2012. It
  crosses the river via the Dã Viên islet and carries six yellow-roofed viewing
  pavilions on outboard pier balconies.
- **The 1908 French steel Warren-truss rail bridges** just upstream (west):
  Bạch Hổ over the north channel and the short Dã Viên rail bridge over the
  south channel. Both are still in use and painted white. A motorbike lane is
  cantilevered on their west side.

## Confirmed
- Road bridge: concrete girder deck, about 24.5 m wide (from the OSM outline and
  photos), opened 2012, six pavilions with yellow roofs and red columns, lit at night.
- Rail bridges: white steel Warren through-trusses, with five spans on Bạch Hổ.
  The bridge lines follow the OSM ways.

## Estimates
- 8 north-channel road spans (about 34.6 m each), counted from piers in photos.
  The press gives 5, which conflicts with the pier count.
- Pavilion size (4.4 m square, two-tier hip roof) and placement: three a side,
  opposite each other at piers 2/4/6.
- Truss height 6 m, panels about 6 m. Dã Viên rail bridge: 2 truss spans plus a short girder span.
- Motorbike lane width (about 2 m) and its side (west, from the 2018 photo).
- Deck height above the water follows the model's compressed vertical scale.

## What changed
- Pavilions moved out of the carriageway onto outboard pier balconies. They are
  now open two-tier yellow-roofed pavilions with warm night lighting.
- The bowed generic trusses were replaced by straight white Warren trusses with
  portals and overhead bracing on concrete piers, plus a motorbike lane.
- The road bridge got wall piers with caps, a haunched girder soffit and pier floodlights.

## What is left
- The islet and south-channel road sections still use the generic piers.
- Road railings and lamp posts are still generic.
- Exact span lengths and pavilion dimensions are unconfirmed (no drawings found).
