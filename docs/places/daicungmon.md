# Đại Cung Môn (in `dainoi`)

Model: the "Đại Cung Môn" block in `src/landmarks/places/dainoi.js` (Imperial City frame: u across, v toward Ngọ
Môn; the gate is centred at u 0, v 100.5, its front facing +v, toward Thái Hòa). It has no place id of its own.
Policy: places being rebuilt are drawn finished, so the gate is drawn whole as it stood before 1947, not as the
foundation that is there in 2026.

## Sources
- khamphahue.com.vn, "Reconstruction of Đại Cung Môn: sufficient evidence from archaeology to history" and
  "Archaeological excavation at Đại Cung Môn completed before restoration" (2025): 5 bays, 3 doors (the middle one
  the emperor's), foundation 23.72 × 12.48 m, front lacquered vermilion and gilded (eight treasures, four sacred
  animals, poems), two 9-bay rear corridors with blue-green glazed tiles to Tả Vu and Hữu Vu; reconstruction with a
  yellow glazed roof, Thanh-stone column bases, brick foundation.
- baovanhoa.vn, "Làm rõ quy mô, kết cấu để phục hồi Đại Cung Môn": "5 gian, trổ 3 cửa"; front toward Thái Hòa all
  timber, red lacquer and gilt; roof "ngói âm ống Hoàng lưu ly"; corridors "ngói thanh lưu ly"; mosaic panels on
  the gable ends, pháp lam figures on the ridge; foundation about 23 × 12 m.
- news.huengaynay.vn, "Đại Cung Môn to be reconstructed" (2026): built 1833 (Minh Mạng), lacquered and gilded 1839;
  burnt February 1947; liên ba panels and door partitions "nhất thi nhất họa"; two boards, "Đại Cung Môn" and
  "Càn Thành Cung"; ridge and hip-end figures in pháp lam and ceramic inlay; Nhật Tinh and Nguyệt Ánh gates either
  side; "relatively well documented in photographs".
- vjol.info.vn, Nghiên cứu và Phát triển (Huế), article 4222: built 1833 as the main entrance of Càn Thành; repainted
  and gilded 1939; destroyed 1947.

## Confirmed
- One gate building of 5 bays with 3 doors in the middle bays, the middle door the emperor's.
- Timber frame, front of red lacquer and gilt with poem-and-picture panels; boards "Đại Cung Môn" and "Càn Thành Cung".
- Yellow glazed yin-yang tiles; enamel figures on the ridge and hip ends; mosaic panels on the gable ends.
- Green glazed corridors either side, running to Tả Vu and Hữu Vu (drawn by the gallery code in the same file).
- Built 1833, burnt 1947; reconstruction planned from 2026.

## Estimated
- **Footprint:** the model keeps the existing foundation, 27 × 11.5 m with the 6 × 4 grid of column bases (5 bays of
  4.4 m, 3 deep of 2.7 m). The excavation gives 23.72 × 12.48 m. Left as it was so the flanking galleries still butt
  onto the foundation ends (u ±13.5); resize both together if this is revisited.
- **Section and heights:** floor 0.7 m above the ground, columns 4.2 m to the eave plate (the inner rows 0.9 m more),
  roof rise 3.1 m: ridge about 8.4 m, the ridge pearl about 9.2 m above the ground. No documented height.
- **Roof form:** one roof, single eave, hip-and-gable (a hip roof with a small vertical gable high on each end),
  inferred from the "mosaic panels on the gable ends". No source gives the form directly; a two-tier or
  chồng-diêm roof is not reported by any source found. Old photographs exist but were not examined.
- **Door screen:** on the front inner row of columns, the 3 middle bays doors (the middle one taller), the end bays
  panelled; the front row stands free as a verandah and the back opens to the Cần Chánh court.
- **Ridge work:** a flaming pearl (hỏa châu) at the middle, two enamel dragons, curled ridge ends: a generic Nguyễn
  composition (as on Thái Hòa), not a documented one.
- Colours follow Thái Hòa's palette (red lacquer, gilt bands, ochre gable walls).

## Disagreements
- Foundation size: 23.72 × 12.48 m (khamphahue, excavation) against about 23 × 12 m (baovanhoa, rounded). The model's
  older 27 × 11.5 m outline matches neither; see Footprint above.
- Rear corridor tiles: "blue glazed" (khamphahue, English) against "thanh lưu ly" (baovanhoa): the same thing; drawn
  green as the other corridors.
